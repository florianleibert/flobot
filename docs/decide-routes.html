the model-routing model — decide, then generate

Every model is a route.

flobot pins a model to each route — a named, documented combination of a role, a source, and a purpose. A teammate (bot) is just a route plus a memory. Pick the right route and the right prompt, and the whole crew lines up.

The documented crew

Four routes ship by default. Each is a deliberate division of labor — cheap where cheap is fine, strong where it matters.

Two more kinds: mesh and direct

mesh/<model> routes come from the live amesh catalog — any ready model served by a mesh peer becomes a route. direct/<model> pins any model on the Meta API; the key stays on this Mac and never reaches the browser. Both are dynamic: whatever the server reports is what you can pin.
